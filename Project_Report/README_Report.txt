2. HOW TO UPDATE STUDENT & COLLEGE PLACEHOLDERS
-----------------------------------------------
Open "BCA_Project_Report.docx" in Microsoft Word and press Ctrl + H (Find & Replace)
to replace the following standardized placeholders with your actual details:

  - [STUDENT NAME]               -> Your Full Name
  - [REGISTER NUMBER]            -> Your University / College Register Number
  - [COLLEGE NAME]               -> Your College Full Name and City
  - [UNIVERSITY NAME]            -> Your Affiliated University Name
  - [GUIDE NAME AND DESIGNATION] -> Your Project Guide's Name and Designation
  - [HOD NAME]                   -> Head of Department Name
  - [ACADEMIC YEAR]              -> e.g., 2025 - 2026 or 2026 - 2027
  - [MONTH AND YEAR]             -> e.g., October 2026

3. UPDATING THE AUTOMATIC TABLE OF CONTENTS IN MICROSOFT WORD
-------------------------------------------------------------
After replacing the placeholders in "BCA_Project_Report.docx":
  1. Go to the "Table of Contents" page.
  2. Right-click anywhere inside the Table of Contents or press Ctrl + A and press F9.
  3. Select "Update entire table" and click OK to refresh all page numbers.

4. EXPORTING TO PDF AFTER EDITING
---------------------------------
Once you have filled in your personal and institutional details in Microsoft Word:
  - In Microsoft Word: Click File -> Save As (or Export) -> PDF (*.pdf).
  - In LibreOffice Writer: Click File -> Export As -> Export as PDF.
